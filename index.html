Interactive v1 · for David’s review

Start with 100 grain. All quotes stay fixed while you exchange through other goods and back to grain in one action. Intermediate goods can be retraded immediately. Your trades do not change quotes.

This prototype uses unlimited liquidity and no fees. Profitable loops can be repeated at these idealized quotes. The corrected-rate scenario separately shows how consistent prices remove the opportunity. Code is MIT-licensed; the accessibility release audit remains pending.

      What this experiment teaches

      An arbitrage loop returns to the same good at known quotes. Its rate product tests whether the relative prices agree. Directional speculation instead relies on an uncertain future price; this prototype has no future price changes.

      Real markets can have fees, limited liquidity, execution risk, and prices that respond to orders. Those effects are excluded here. “Corrected rates” demonstrates consistent prices but does not simulate a process of price convergence. This prototype’s numerical balance limit is one billion grain.

      What this experiment teaches

      Arbitrage, not a forecast

      A closed loop returns to the same good at known quotes. Its rate product tests whether relative prices agree. Directional speculation relies on an uncertain future price; this lab has no future price changes.

      A deliberately simple market

      Real markets can have fees, limited liquidity, execution risk, and prices that respond to orders. Those effects are excluded. Corrected quotes demonstrate consistency, not a process of convergence. The numerical balance limit is one billion grain.
